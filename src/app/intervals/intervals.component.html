How to Play

  1. Click Play a note — the note will be highlighted in red on the piano keyboard.
  2. Click Play the second note to hear the note you need to identify.
  3. Use the piano keyboard to guess the correct note.
    • If your guess is correct, the key will turn green.
    • If it’s incorrect, try again!
  4. You can replay any note at any time to help find the correct interval.

Practice and challenge yourself to improve your ear for intervals!
